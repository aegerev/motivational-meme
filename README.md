# Motivational Meme

## About
This happened during Episode 308 of ONE PIECE. Luffy was on the verge of death. However, remembering the promise he made to a friend (Nico Robin), he mustered all of his strength, and PUMMELED Rob Lucci into the ground, winning his friend back. <br/>

This is why I love Luffy. He doesn't give up. Whoever's not a coward, who doesn't drop anchor, that person will find land.

This project explores the core principle that true strength lies not in the ability to deliver a powerful blow, but in the resilience to withstand adversity and continue moving forward. 

## Key Message:

* Focus on Resilience: The emphasis is on the ability to "GET hit" and "TAKE" punishment while maintaining progress. 
* Winning Mindset: True winning is defined by perseverance in the face of challenges, not just the ability to inflict damage.
